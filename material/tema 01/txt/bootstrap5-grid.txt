Bootstrap 5.3 — Grid System
Fuente: https://getbootstrap.com/docs/5.3/layout/grid/
========================================================

INTRODUCCIÓN
============
El grid de Bootstrap usa contenedores, filas y columnas para layoutear y alinear contenido.
Está construido con flexbox y es completamente responsive.

ESTRUCTURA BÁSICA
=================
<div class="container">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col">Column</div>
    <div class="col">Column</div>
    <div class="col">Column</div>
  </div>
</div>

CÓMO FUNCIONA
=============
- 12 columnas por fila
- 6 breakpoints responsive
- Gutters (espaciado) configurables
- Basado en flexbox

BREAKPOINTS
===========
xs  (Extra small)  < 576px   .col-
sm  (Small)        >= 576px  .col-sm-  (Container max: 540px)
md  (Medium)       >= 768px  .col-md-  (Container max: 720px)
lg  (Large)        >= 992px  .col-lg-  (Container max: 960px)
xl  (Extra large)  >= 1200px .col-xl-  (Container max: 1140px)
xxl (XX large)     >= 1400px .col-xxl- (Container max: 1320px)

COLUMNAS IGUALES (auto-layout)
==============================
<div class="container text-center">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col">1 of 2</div>
    <div class="col">2 of 2</div>
  </div>
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col">1 of 3</div>
    <div class="col">2 of 3</div>
    <div class="col">3 of 3</div>
  </div>
</div>

COLUMNAS CON ANCHO FIJO
========================
<div class="container text-center">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col">1 of 3</div>
    <div class="col-6">2 of 3 (wider)</div>
    <div class="col">3 of 3</div>
  </div>
</div>

STACKED TO HORIZONTAL
=====================
<div class="container text-center">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-sm-8">col-sm-8</div>  <!-- 8/12 desde 576px -->
    <div class="col-sm-4">col-sm-4</div>  <!-- 4/12 desde 576px -->
  </div>
</div>

MIX AND MATCH
=============
<!-- Stack en móvil, columnas en desktop -->
<div class="container text-center">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-md-8">.col-md-8</div>
    <div class="col-6 col-md-4">.col-6 .col-md-4</div>
  </div>
  <!-- 33% wide en móvil, 33% wide en desktop -->
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-6 col-md-4">.col-6 .col-md-4</div>
    <div class="col-6 col-md-4">.col-6 .col-md-4</div>
    <div class="col-6 col-md-4">.col-6 .col-md-4</div>
  </div>
  <!-- Siempre 50% -->
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-6">.col-6</div>
    <div class="col-6">.col-6</div>
  </div>
</div>

ROW COLUMNS
===========
<!-- 2 columnas por fila -->
<div class="row row-cols-2">
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
</div>

<!-- Responsive: 1 col en móvil, 2 en sm, 4 en md -->
<div class="row row-cols-1 row-cols-sm-2 row-cols-md-4">
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
  <div class="col">Column</div>
</div>

GUTTERS
=======
<!-- g-3 = gutter horizontal y vertical de 1rem -->
<div class="row g-3">
  <div class="col-md-6">...</div>
  <div class="col-md-6">...</div>
</div>

<!-- Sin gutters -->
<div class="row g-0">...</div>

<!-- Solo horizontal -->
<div class="row gx-5">...</div>

<!-- Solo vertical -->
<div class="row gy-2">...</div>

VARIABLES SASS (para referencia)
================================
$grid-columns:      12;
$grid-gutter-width: 1.5rem;
$grid-row-columns:  6;

$grid-breakpoints: (
  xs: 0,
  sm: 576px,
  md: 768px,
  lg: 992px,
  xl: 1200px,
  xxl: 1400px
);
